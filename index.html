Chapter I

About Me

I lost my dad as a teenager. Not long after, a mentor asked me one question that has quietly shaped every choice since: “Now that your life is in your own hands, will you work to make it a quality one? What quality choices are you going to make that define you?”

That question became a career. I build software quality for a living — automation, pipelines, coverage, culture — but underneath it, it’s the same question, asked over and over: is this the quality choice?

Born and raised in Utah, I still spend as much time outdoors as I can. When I’m not testing software, I’m usually making something with my hands — hats, art, woodworking — anything where quality is something you can actually hold.
